• A bit about us
    • Oyapock Cyber Shop saw the light in 2009, aiming at helping people notably with administrative and digital procedures before administrations and public entities; to do so, a know-how and equipment to connect to the Internet are available; also, other services are offered, including a dining and coffee shop, together with a bazaar. In the past, the place was a mini-market (Super Marko), born in 1999; before that, it was a playroom (Garden Games), which opened back in 1993! Behind these three projects, there was always one lady: Sylvianne Eloïse, born in Saint-Georges-de-l´Oyapock.
